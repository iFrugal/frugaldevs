// BUILD VS BUY

A calculation, not a preference.

if (build) {

  • b.01The capability is your moat — renting it means renting your advantage.
  • b.02Three-year TCO of the vendor exceeds a senior build, run cost included.
  • b.03Lock-in is high: proprietary formats, punitive exit terms, no data portability.
  • b.04The vendor covers 60% of the workflow — and the other 40% is where your money is made.

}

else buy {

  • y.01It's plumbing — undifferentiated capability the market sells at commodity price.
  • y.02Time to value wins: the vendor ships in weeks, a build lands in quarters.
  • y.03You'd own a system you can't staff — maintenance outlives the builder.
  • y.04The vendor's scale (compliance, uptime, security) isn't worth rebuilding.

}

Every engagement opens with this analysis — three-year total cost of ownership, lock-in priced, integration cost on the sheet. About one in four reports concludes you shouldn't hire us to build anything. You get it in writing either way.
